Creating a professional invoice

This free invoice generator lets freelancers, shopkeepers and small businesses create a clean PDF invoice in a few minutes. Fill in your business details, the client's details, invoice number and dates, add line items with quantity and rate, set a tax or GST percentage and an optional discount, and download the invoice as a PDF. You can switch the currency between rupees, dollars, euros and pounds.

A GST tax invoice normally shows the supplier's name, address and GSTIN, a unique invoice number and date, the buyer's details, a description of the goods or services with HSN or SAC code, quantity, taxable value, the tax rate and tax amount, and the place of supply. Use the Description and Notes fields to add codes and other details your invoice needs.

How to use it

  1. Enter your business details and the client's details.
  2. Set the invoice number, invoice date and due date.
  3. Add items with description, quantity and rate, then set the tax percentage and any discount.
  4. Add payment terms in the notes, preview the invoice and download the PDF, or share it on WhatsApp.

Good to know

  • Use a continuous invoice number series so that no number is repeated.
  • Write payment details such as UPI ID or bank account in the notes so clients can pay quickly.
  • Keep a copy of every invoice you issue for your accounts and GST returns.
  • Businesses above the government's turnover limit must follow e-invoicing rules. Check with your accountant whether they apply to you.

Frequently asked questions

Is my invoice data uploaded?

No. The invoice is generated in your browser and a draft is saved only on your device. NxtCraft does not receive it.

Is this a replacement for accounting software?

No. It is a simple invoice maker. For bookkeeping, inventory and GST filing, use accounting software or consult an accountant.

Can I issue invoices in dollars or euros?

Yes. Choose the currency in the invoice details section.
